Muzhi-3D
is an upcoming (work in progress) curve based 3d polymodeller with a focus on ease of use and speed of creation.
it aims to be parametric and able to create variations of the same model.
it is currently in the early stages of development and is not yet available for public use.
Muzhi 3d will also be able to suggest topology patterns similar to blenders grid fill.

Curves. topology. meshes. procedural.
